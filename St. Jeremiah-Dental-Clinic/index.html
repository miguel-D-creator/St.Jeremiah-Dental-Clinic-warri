Recovery & Aftercare

After Tooth Extraction

  • Bite firmly on the gauze for 45 minutes to stop bleeding.
  • Avoid spitting, smoking, or using straws for the first 24 hours.
  • Stick to cold or lukewarm soft foods (pudding, yogurt, soup).

After Scaling & Whitening

  • Avoid highly pigmented foods (coffee, red wine) for 48 hours.
  • Use a soft-bristled brush to maintain the results gently.
